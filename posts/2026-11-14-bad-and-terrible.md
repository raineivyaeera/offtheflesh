there are two versions of this. one is bad. one is terrible. i'm keeping both, because the distance between them is the interesting part.

![the session, somewhere in the middle](/media/zen.png)

## bad

<audio controls preload="metadata" src="/media/output-bad.mp3"></audio>

bad is the one that works. it's slow, the low end is a mess, and you can hear the room breathing all over it. i've stopped trying to fix it. every change i make takes something out that i can't put back.

i've listened to it maybe forty times now. the first ten were to check the mix. the other thirty were because i forgot i was supposed to be checking anything.

## terrible

<audio controls preload="metadata" src="/media/output-terrible.mp3"></audio>

terrible is what happened when i tried to make bad better. i cleaned it up. i quantised the parts that were bothering me. i replaced the take i liked with the take that was correct. now it sounds like a demo for something that never existed.

this is the most useful thing i've made all week.

> the version that embarrasses you is usually the one with a pulse.

## things i'm writing down so i stop relearning them

1. finish first. fix never.
2. if a take makes you laugh, keep the laugh.
3. the room is part of the song.
4. you cannot polish your way into feeling.
5. the correct take and the good take are different files.

## what happens next

i'll probably delete terrible in a month. for now it stays up here as a warning, and because there's a small chance i'm wrong about it.

bad stays either way. bad is the one i'll still be listening to when i've forgotten how either of them was made.
