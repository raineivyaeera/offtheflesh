i don't have a room for this. i have a folder, and the folder has a wallpaper in it, and that's close enough for now.

![a painting i keep coming back to](/media/wallpaper.webp)

## the room

the plan was a small room with one window and nothing on the walls except this. a desk under the window. the kind of room where you can hear weather. i've been building it in my head for a while, mostly at night, mostly instead of sleeping.

it has a song now, which is more than the room has.

### came too early

<audio controls preload="metadata" src="/media/came-too-early.mp3"></audio>

i made this before the room existed. it's too long and it doesn't go anywhere, which is exactly what a room with no furniture should sound like. there's a part near the end where it almost resolves and then decides not to. i've tried to write a better ending four times. the not-resolving is the ending.

## inventory

what the room has:

- one window, painted shut
- a desk that doesn't fit through the door
- this wallpaper
- a song that came too early

what it doesn't have:

- a door
- a floor
- anyone in it

> you can want a place before you know what it's for.

## notes on the wallpaper

i keep coming back to the light in it. it's the light of a room i've never been in, at a time of day i can't name. i don't want to live there. i want to sit in it for an hour and then leave without saying anything.

the colours are almost the palette of this website, which is either a coincidence or the reason i downloaded it in the first place.

i'll keep adding to this one. eventually it'll be a real room, or a real song, or i'll forget and start a different one. that's fine too.
