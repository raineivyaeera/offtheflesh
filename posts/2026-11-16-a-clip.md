i've been opening this file every few days, moving one thing, and closing it again. it's forty seconds long and i still don't know what it is.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/media/clip.jpg" src="/media/clip.mp4"></video>

## what it started as

a test, mostly. i wanted to see how long i could hold a single frame before it stopped feeling like a mistake. the answer, it turns out, is about eleven seconds. after that it just looks like the video is broken, which is not the same thing.

so i cut it up. then i put it back together in the wrong order. then i put it back in the right order and hated it.

> the version i like is the one where nothing happens for long enough that you start looking at the edges.

## notes while watching it back

- the first shot is doing almost nothing, and that's on purpose
- the sound was recorded in the same room a week later
- there is a click at 00:18 that i have decided is a feature
- every time i try to add a second idea, the whole thing falls over

![a still from the clip](/media/clip.jpg)

## where it's going

nowhere, probably. or here, which is a kind of nowhere. i keep it around because it's the only thing i've made this month that doesn't explain itself.

the room it was shot in doesn't exist anymore. the light was borrowed from a neighbour's window and the whole thing was over before i figured out what i was looking at. i think that's why it works. it doesn't know it's a video yet.

if i ever finish it i'll put the finished version in this post. for now, forty seconds is enough.
